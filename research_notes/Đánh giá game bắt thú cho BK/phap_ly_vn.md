## Q1. Nghị định 147/2024/NĐ-CP — does the game count as a "trò chơi điện tử trên mạng", what licence, what minor protections?

### Takeaway
Decree 147 has **no exemption** for free, non-commercial, closed or educational games: any online game a Vietnamese enterprise offers to players over a network falls under it. Once players interact with each other through BK's server (party, friends, visible players), it is a **G1 game**, which needs a ministry licence plus a per-game release decision. That is far heavier than the G2 path (provincial certificate plus release notice) available to a single-player-vs-server version. Whatever the type, the hard rules for minors apply: 60 minutes per game and 180 minutes per day for under-18s, a health warning every 30 minutes, an age rating, and account verification by Vietnamese phone number. For under-16s, a parent registers the account. Since 01/7/2026 these rules carry fines under Decree 174/2026.

### Inferences
- **Classification of BK's game.** The single-player phase (catch creatures, fight bosses against the server) is G2. Once players see each other, party up and add friends through BK's server, it becomes **G1**, whether or not money is involved. Preset-only chat does not change this: the test is player-to-player interaction via the server.
- **"Closed, free, students only" is not a legal exemption** under any source found. The decree regulates the act of providing an online game to players. The closed audience lowers the practical enforcement risk, not the legal requirement. A lawyer, or an informal query to MOCST/ABEI or the provincial Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism, should confirm whether a non-commercial in-app learning mini-game is treated as "dịch vụ trò chơi điện tử trên mạng".
- **Business registration.** BK's legal entity probably needs game services in its business lines (VSIC 6190/6209-type codes; the code is not verified) to obtain even the G2 certificate. A tutoring company registered only for education services would not qualify as is.
- **Phased approach to reduce exposure.** Launch as G2 (no player interaction) under a provincial certificate. Defer multiplayer, which needs G1 and its ministry licence, or watch whether the draft amendment creates an easier educational-game track.
- **Playtime cap for design.** For safety, design to the stricter draft rule: 60 minutes/day total for under-16s, and 60 minutes per game per day for 16–17-year-olds. Count the cap server-side in Postgres (VN timezone), not on the client.

- Full verbatim text of Arts. 36–57 could not be fetched (thuvienphapluat returned 403). Clause-level numbering for the playtime rule (Art. 43/52?) is unconfirmed.

## Q2. Personal data of children — Decree 13/2023 → Personal Data Protection Law 2025 + Decree 356/2025

### Takeaway
Decree 13/2023 is **no longer the governing text**. Since 01/01/2026, the Personal Data Protection Law (Luật BVDLCN) No. 91/2025/QH15 and its implementing Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP apply. Under them, children's data gets special protection, and some processing needs consent from **both the child (7+) and the parent**. Data that tracks online behaviour or activity, and location data, are listed as *sensitive*, which can cancel the small-business grace period. Separately, Luật Trẻ em 2016 / Decree 56/2017 require consent from parent and child (7+) before any child's private information (name, school, class, results, friends…) is put online.

### Cited Findings
- Law 91/2025/QH15 was passed 26/06/2025. Both the law and Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP took effect **01/01/2026**, and Decree 356 replaces Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP. — [LuatVietnam – NĐ 356](https://luatvietnam.vn/thong-tin/nghi-dinh-356-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-chi-tiet-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-422896-d1.html); [EY legal alert 03/2026](https://www.ey.com/vi_vn/technical/tax/tax-and-law-updates/nghi-dinh-so-356-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-chi-tiet-mot-so-dieu-va-bien-phap-thi-hanh-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan)
- **Children (Law 91, Art. 24):** processing a child's data for the purpose of publishing or disclosing information about their private life requires consent from **both the child aged 7+ and their legal representative**. For children under 7, the representative alone consents. Processing must stop if consent is withdrawn or if a competent authority finds it harms the child. — [LuatVietnam English text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html)
- **Sensitive data (Decree 356, Art. 4)** includes:
  - location determined via positioning services;
  - "dữ liệu theo dõi hành vi, hoạt động sử dụng dịch vụ viễn thông, mạng xã hội" (data tracking behaviour and activity on telecom and social-network services);
  - biometric, financial and health data.
  
  Sensitive data requires access controls and explicit notice that the data is sensitive. — [LuatVietnam – NĐ 356](https://luatvietnam.vn/thong-tin/nghi-dinh-356-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-chi-tiet-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-422896-d1.html)
- **Consent (Decree 356, Art. 6):** consent must be verifiable (written, recorded call, SMS, email or electronic platform), and **default or pre-ticked consent is banned**. — [LuatVietnam – NĐ 356](https://luatvietnam.vn/thong-tin/nghi-dinh-356-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-chi-tiet-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-422896-d1.html)
- **Impact assessment and DPO:**
  - Impact-assessment dossiers (Law Arts. 21–22; Decree 356 Arts. 18–19) are filed within 60 days of starting the activity.
  - DPO qualifications are set in Decree 356 Arts. 13–16.
  - Breaches involving sensitive data must be reported within 72 hours.
  
  — [LuatVietnam – NĐ 356](https://luatvietnam.vn/thong-tin/nghi-dinh-356-2025-nd-cp-quy-dinh-chi-tiet-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-422896-d1.html)
- **SME grace (Law 91, Art. 38(2)–(3)):**
  - Small enterprises and startups may choose not to apply Arts. 21, 22 (impact assessments) and Art. 33(2) (designated DPO staff) for 5 years from 01/01/2026.
  - Micro-enterprises and household businesses are exempt from these.
  - **Exception: the grace does not apply to those providing data-processing services, those directly processing sensitive personal data, or those processing data of a large number of subjects.**
  
  — [LuatVietnam English text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html); [thuvienphapluat summary via search](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/phap-luat/mot-so-luu-y-khi-luat-bao-ve-du-lieu-ca-nhan-2025-co-hieu-luc-danh-cho-ho-kinh-doanh-doanh-nghiep-s-924781-244918.html)
- **Online/social services (Law 91, Art. 29):** providers must:
  - notify users of data collection;
  - not require images of ID documents for authentication;
  - offer a way to refuse cookies and tracking;
  - publish a clear privacy policy.
  
  — [LuatVietnam English text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html)
- **Fines:** the cap for cross-border-transfer violations is 5% of the organisation's prior-year revenue (Law 91, Art. 8(4)). — [LuatVietnam English text](https://english.luatvietnam.vn/dan-su/law-on-personal-data-protection-law-no-91-2025-qh15-405135-d1.html)
- **Children's private information (Luật Trẻ em 2016, Art. 54; Decree 56/2017, Arts. 33–35):**
  - Art. 54 requires organisations that provide online services or organise online activities to ensure children's safety and privacy.

  - "Trẻ em" under Luật Trẻ em means under 16. For 16–17-year-olds, ordinary personal-data rules apply.

### Inferences
- **Which data in BK's game is regulated:**
  - **Player-visible data** (name or nickname shown to others, class or school, friend lists, leaderboards, creature collection linked to a named student) is a child's private information under Decree 56, Art. 33. Showing it to other students is arguably "đưa lên môi trường mạng". The safe course is **parent + child (7+) consent**, collected in a verifiable form with no pre-ticked boxes.
  - **Gameplay telemetry** (session logs, behaviour tracking) may count as sensitive "behaviour-tracking" data under Decree 356, Art. 4. If so, BK would lose the 5-year SME grace and need an impact assessment (DPIA) and a designated person. Whether game telemetry inside BK's own app fits a definition worded around "telecom services and social networks" is ambiguous. **Ask a lawyer.**
  - **Location:** do not collect GPS. The game needs none, and location is sensitive data.
  - **Preset-only chat** is a good minimisation measure. Chat logs still count as personal data, so set a short retention period.
- **Minimisation pays.** Separate game data from academic data where possible, and use pseudonymous nicknames rather than real names in multiplayer views.
- **Overlap with Decree 147.** Art. 56 already demands phone-verified accounts and parent registration for under-16s. That data collection is lawful under PDPL Art. 17-type legal-obligation grounds (unverified article), but it still needs notice.

### Gaps
- Exact Law 91 article on the lawful bases other than consent: not fetched.
- Whether Decree 356 sets a separate age-verification method for children: not found.
- Thresholds for "large number of data subjects" (cancelling the SME grace): not found. A few thousand students may or may not cross it.
- Precise fine amounts for violations involving children's data under the 2026 penalty framework: not found.

## Q3. Virtual currency "xu", real rewards, gacha / loot boxes

### Takeaway
This is the **highest-risk design point**. Decree 147, Art. 57 forbids in-game virtual items, virtual units and reward points from being converted into money, cards, vouchers **or any physical items with value outside the game**. It also forbids attaching prize promotions (cash or real goods outside the game) to the game. If BK's game *pays out xu that can be redeemed for real rewards*, it directly hits Art. 57(3)–(4) **if the game is a regulated online game**. Separately, xu earned through study and redeemed for gifts looks like a commercial promotion / loyalty scheme under the Commercial Law and Decree 81/2018. No specific Vietnamese loot-box or gacha rule for minors was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Decree 147, Art. 57:**
  - (1) Virtual items, units and points may only be created as reported in the licence or certificate dossier.
  - (2) Players may use points or units only to buy or exchange virtual items in the same game.
  - (3) The enterprise **may not attach prize promotions (cash or real goods outside the game) to the game's interface or features**.
  - (4) Items, units and points **may not be converted back into money, prepaid telecom cards, bank cards, shopping cards, game cards, gift cards or items with transaction value outside the game**.
  - (5) Players may not buy or sell items, units or points among themselves.
  
  — [Pháp luật & Phát triển](https://phapluatphattrien.vn/quy-dinh-ve-vat-pham-ao-diem-thuong-trong-tro-choi-dien-tu-tu-25-12-2024-d2659.html); [Xây dựng chính sách – Chính phủ](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/nghi-dinh-147-2024-nd-cp-khong-mua-ban-vat-pham-ao-giua-nhung-nguoi-choi-tro-choi-dien-tu-voi-nhau-119241113161443995.htm)
- Trading virtual items carries a 2–3 million VND fine for players from 01/7/2026 (Decree 174/2026). — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/tin-van-ban-moi/tu-01-7-2026-mua-ban-vat-pham-ao-trong-game-phat-den-3-trieu-dong-186-109743-article.html)
- Decree 147 makes casino-style and card-based games ineligible for licensing. — [VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/vietnam-tightens-online-gaming-rules-bans-casino-style-and-card-based-games-2346909.html); [Yogonet](https://www.yogonet.com/international/news/2024/12/05/87586-vietnam-39s-new-decree-147-enforces-stricter-regulations-on-online-gaming-industry)
- **Promotion law (Decree 81/2018, guiding the Commercial Law):**
  - Promotions with total prize value under 100 million VND do not require the notification procedure.
  - Otherwise, notify the provincial Department of Industry and Trade at least 3 working days before starting.
  - The value of promotional goods may not exceed 50% of the value of the promoted goods or services.
  
  — [search summary of NĐ 81/2018 (vbpl.vn)](https://vbpl.vn/bocongthuong/Pages/vbpq-toanvan.aspx?ItemID=129582&dvid=218); [dichvucong.gov.vn](https://dichvucong.gov.vn/p/home/dvc-chi-tiet-cau-hoi.html?id=13721&row_limit=1)

### Inferences
- **Recommended design: keep two currencies.**
  - **Wallet xu (real-reward currency)** is earned only from learning outcomes, outside the game, as a tuition/loyalty perk.
  - **Game currency** (e.g. "đá", or creature points) is earned and spent only inside the game and **never converts into wallet xu or real prizes**.
  
  Paying wallet xu out of the game would contradict Art. 57(3)–(4) once the game is under Decree 147.
- **Gacha / random rewards.** Even with no real money, avoid random draws whose prizes have real value. Randomness with external value edges toward "khuyến mại mang tính may rủi" (needs registration) and toward gambling concepts. Random *cosmetic* creature drops with no external value are lower risk.
- **Tradeable creatures.** Do not allow player-to-player trading or gifting of in-game items or currency. Art. 57(5) bans buying and selling; gifting is a grey area, so prefer a ban.
- **Has the existing wallet-xu programme been reviewed under Decree 81/2018?** If the total value of redeemed prizes exceeds 100 million VND per programme, BK may need to notify the Department of Industry and Trade, and the 50% value cap may bind. This is unverified for education services. **Ask a lawyer.**

### Gaps
- No Vietnamese rule found that targets loot boxes or gacha for minors specifically.
- How Decree 81/2018 applies to a tutoring centre's points-for-gifts scheme for its own enrolled students: not confirmed. A draft amendment to Decree 81 exists ([LuatVietnam draft](https://luatvietnam.vn/thuong-mai/du-thao-nghi-dinh-sua-doi-bo-sung-nghi-dinh-81-2018-nd-cp-ve-xuc-tien-thuong-mai-414723-d10.html)), but its status was not checked.
- Whether Art. 57 bites if the game itself is held not to be a regulated online game: this depends on Q1's unresolved exemption question.

## Q4. Child protection, screen time and MOET rules on tutoring centres (Thông tư 29/2024)

### Takeaway
Luật Trẻ em 2016 (Art. 54) and Decree 56/2017 (Arts. 33–35) apply to BK as an organisation providing online services to children: it must ensure their safety and privacy online, get consent before posting private information, and show warnings. Thông tư 29/2024/TT-BGDĐT on extra tutoring (effective 14/02/2025) regulates teaching, registration and disclosure, and contains **nothing about games**. Since 01/7/2025, though, the centre must be a registered business that publicly discloses its subjects, schedule and fees. No MOET circular on student game time was found.

### Cited Findings
- **Luật Trẻ em 2016, Art. 54:** organisations that manage or provide information and communication products or services, or organise activities online, must take measures to ensure children's safety and private life. Parents and teachers must educate children on online self-protection. — [Tây Ninh PBGDPL](https://snnmt.tayninh.gov.vn/tuyen-truyen-van-ban-phap-luat/bao-ve-tre-em-tren-moi-truong-mang-985754); [Lập pháp](https://lapphap.vn/Pages/tintuc/tinchitiet.aspx?tintucid=208110)
- **Decree 56/2017, Arts. 33–35:**
  - Art. 33 lists the protected information.
  - Online service providers need consent from the parent and the child (7+) before posting a child's private information.
  - They must use safety tools and show **warning messages when a child provides or changes private information**.
  
  — [thuvienphapluat](https://thuvienphapluat.vn/chinh-sach-phap-luat-moi/vn/chong-lua-dao/49288/cac-bien-phap-bao-ve-thong-tin-bi-mat-doi-song-rieng-tu-cho-tre-em-tren-moi-truong-mang); [LSVN](https://lsvn.vn/cac-bien-phap-bao-ve-thong-tin-bi-mat-doi-song-rieng-tu-cho-tre-em-tren-moi-truong-mang-1685761920-a131160.html)
- **Thông tư 29/2024/TT-BGDĐT (effective 14/02/2025):**
  - Organisations offering paid extra tutoring outside school must register a business.
  - They must publicly disclose, online or at the centre, the subjects, hours, location, format, schedule, tutors and fees.
  - Teachers may not give paid tutoring to their own school students.
  
  — [Xây dựng chính sách – Chính phủ](https://xaydungchinhsach.chinhphu.vn/thong-tu-so-29-2024-tt-bgddt-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them-119250103212125862.htm); [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/giao-duc/thong-tu-29-2024-tt-bgddt-cua-bo-giao-duc-va-dao-tao-quy-dinh-ve-day-them-hoc-them-383552-d1.html)

### Inferences
- **Thông tư 29 does not restrict an in-app game.** The indirect link is the centre's business registration (Q1): adding a game may require new business lines.
- **Child-protection duties in practice:**
  - Parental consent flow.
  - Warning text in the profile or name-edit UI.
  - Moderation and reporting tools (a "report player" button, the ability to block).
  - Preset-only chat already removes most grooming and bullying risk.
- **Screen time.** No MOET rule found. The binding numbers come from Decree 147 (Q1). Pedagogically, cap well below them, e.g. tie playtime to completed study tasks.

### Gaps
- MOET circulars or guidance on students' game time or digital-wellbeing: none found in this search, which was not exhaustive.
- Penalty amounts for breaching Decree 56 online-privacy duties: not searched.
- Thông tư 29's full text was not fetched. The date for the business-registration requirement (01/7/2025?) is from memory and unverified.

## Q5. Practical compliance checklist for a small education company

### Takeaway
Ranked by certainty:

- **Definitely applies (whenever the game is live):**
  - PDPL 91/2025 + Decree 356/2025: consent, notice, no pre-ticked boxes, security, breach reporting.
  - Luật Trẻ em / Decree 56: parent + child (7+) consent for showing private information; warnings.
- **Applies if the game is a "trò chơi điện tử trên mạng" (no exemption found, so treat as applying), Decree 147:**
  - G2 certificate now; G1 licence once multiplayer.
  - Phone-verified accounts; parent registration for under-16s.
  - 60/180-minute caps for under-18s; 30-minute health warning; age rating displayed.
  - Art. 57 ban on converting game currency to real value.
  - Decree 174/2026 fines.
- **Might apply:**
  - Decree 81/2018 promotion rules for the wallet-xu reward scheme.
  - DPIA/DPO duties if game telemetry counts as sensitive "behaviour tracking" data.
  - The stricter draft amendment (60 minutes/day total for under-16s) if adopted.
  - The draft's possible educational-game support track.
- **Doesn't apply:**
  - The casino/card-game ban (not a casino game).
  - Rules on in-game payment systems (no real money).
  - Public game-shop rules (điểm trò chơi công cộng).
  - Thông tư 29 (no game content).
  - Esports provisions.

### Cited Findings
- (See Q1–Q4 for sources of each obligation.) Fines for non-verification: 20–60 million VND. — [LuatVietnam](https://luatvietnam.vn/hanh-chinh/tai-khoan-game-khong-xac-thuc-so-dien-thoai-bi-phat-bao-nhieu-570-109975-article.html)

### Inferences — checklist
1. **Legal opinion first (needs a lawyer):** does a free, closed game inside a tutoring app need a G2 certificate or G1 licence? Can BK stay G2 for now? Does BK's business registration need new lines? Possibly send an informal written query to the provincial Department of Culture, Sports and Tourism (G2) or MOCST/ABEI (G1).
2. **Stage the product:**
   - v1 single-player vs server (G2).
   - Multiplayer only after the G1 path is clear, or after the Decree 147 amendment is adopted (it may help educational games).
3. **Accounts:**
   - Reuse student accounts, but add a **Vietnamese phone number verification** (parent's phone for under-16s).
   - Record date of birth; parent registers or consents for under-16s.
   - Keep player data while active plus 6 months.
4. **Time caps, enforced server-side (Postgres, Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh day):**
   - Under-18s: 60 minutes per game per day; 180 minutes total across BK games.
   - Design for 60 minutes/day total for under-16s in anticipation of the draft.
   - Health warning every 30 minutes, with the exact statutory text.
5. **Age rating:** self-classify (likely 00+ or 12+). Keep creature combat cartoonish, with no weapons/gore if aiming for 00+. Show the rating continuously on the game screen and in promos.
6. **Currencies:** wallet xu and game currency are separate. **No game → real-reward conversion**, no player trading, no random prizes with real value.
7. **Consent and privacy:**
   - Verifiable parent + child (7+) consent for showing nickname, class or friends to other players; no pre-ticked boxes.
   - Privacy notice for the game.
   - Pseudonymous display names; no GPS.
   - Short retention for chat and telemetry logs; access controls; a 72-hour breach plan.
8. **Child safety:** preset chat only (already planned), block/report, warnings on profile edits, an adult moderator assigned.
9. **Promotions:** have the lawyer check whether the wallet-xu redemption scheme needs Decree 81/2018 notification (threshold 100 million VND per programme, 50% value cap).
10. **Monitor:** the Decree 147 amendment (MOCST draft, 07/2026) and any Decree 356 or MOCST guidance on educational games.

### Gaps
- Cost and timeline of obtaining a G1 licence for a small company: not researched. game.gov.vn gives 20 days processing time only.
- Financial-capacity and personnel thresholds for G1/G2 (Decree 147, Mục 3): not retrieved.
- Real-world enforcement against small closed or educational games: no cases found.
