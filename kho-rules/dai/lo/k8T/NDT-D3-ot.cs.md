## DE
Giải phương trình: $\dfrac{x+9}{2000}+\dfrac{x+11}{1998}=\dfrac{x+13}{1996}+\dfrac{x+15}{1994}$.
## SACH
$\dfrac{x+9}{2000}+\dfrac{x+11}{1998}=\dfrac{x+13}{1996}+\dfrac{x+15}{1994}$
$\Leftrightarrow \dfrac{x+9}{2000}+1+\dfrac{x+11}{1998}+1=\dfrac{x+13}{1996}+1+\dfrac{x+15}{1994}+1$
$\Leftrightarrow \dfrac{x+9+2000}{2000}+\dfrac{x+11+1998}{1998}=\dfrac{x+13+1996}{1996}+\dfrac{x+15+1994}{1994}$
$\Leftrightarrow \dfrac{x+2009}{2000}+\dfrac{x+2009}{1998}-\dfrac{x+2009}{1996}-\dfrac{x+2009}{1994}=0$
